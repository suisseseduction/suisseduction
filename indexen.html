The process

The coaching step-by-step

01 · My approach

A method built on experience

With 12 years of passionate study and practice, I have achieved strong results in my own dating life. For several years, I have also coached clients and helped them make progress toward their dating goals.

Drawing on this experience, I have developed a practical, straightforward approach and focused exercises tailored to your goals.

02 · Intro call

A confidential first conversation

In a free, no-obligation 20-minute video call, we discuss what you would like to improve and see whether coaching is right for you.

Everything you share with me will remain confidential.

03 · Personalized program

Coaching shaped around you

If you decide to continue, I will build a programme around your goals.

You will also receive a practical guide to my method, covering each step from starting a conversation to building a deeper emotional connection and an intimate relationship.

04 · Practice

Learn through real practice

We meet in Lausanne or Geneva, during the day or evening, depending on the session.

I’ll show you how to create interest as a conversation unfolds and explain what makes an exchange engaging and natural. Then you’ll practise step by step, with my guidance and clear feedback at every stage. We adapt the pace to your comfort level so you can understand what to improve after each interaction.

05 · Review and next steps

Progress you can build on

Join hundreds of clients who have made progress in their relationships. Each session helps you practise, recognize your progress, and feel more at ease starting conversations and creating a relaxed, comfortable interaction with a woman.

After each session, we review what went well, identify what to refine and set clear next steps. You leave with practical tools and greater confidence to keep progressing on your own.

Services

Everything I can do for you.

Simplified Theory

From the start, you receive a practical guide to a clear, step-by-step approach to dating, building a connection, and moving a conversation forward. I have developed it over the past five years from my own experience.

Increase Your Value

We work on the details that shape an authentic first impression: your clothing, posture, body language, voice, and online presence. The goal is to present yourself in a way that feels consistent with who you are.

Motivation

I help you take action, manage the fear of rejection, and learn from each experience. We move at your pace, with concrete goals and encouragement to help you practise consistently.

Dating Apps

We improve your Tinder or Bumble profile together, from choosing photos to writing your bio and opening messages. The goal is to show who you are and encourage natural conversations without pretending to be someone else.

Nightgame & Daygame

Practise in real settings in Lausanne or Geneva, during the day or evening. I can demonstrate an approach first, then you try it with my feedback. Afterwards, we review what worked and what you can improve.

Text Game

We review your conversations and work on tone, timing, and when to suggest meeting. You learn to write natural messages that sound like you and help move the conversation forward.
